# Contributing

Thank you for helping small business owners.

## Ask for a new pack

Open an issue with the **New pack request** template. Tell us the business type and 3 jobs that waste your time.

## Fix or improve a skill

1. Open an issue with the **Skill fix** template, or send a pull request.
2. Keep each skill in this order: What it does, What I need from you, Steps, Output (a literal template), Rules with the reason, a messy worked example, Tips, Quick paste version.
3. Keep every number (word limits, day counts). Do not replace them with words like "short" or "soon".
4. No invented prices, claims, or promises. Skills draft only. They never send.
5. Use simple English. Write for owners who may read English as a second language.

## Please do not

- Add real customer data, names, phone numbers, or keys.
- Add legal, medical, tax or financial advice.
- Add a different brand name inside skill files.
