# Before-and-After Posts — your work is your best ad

Homeowners trust photos of real jobs, near them, more than any ad. One post per finished job, in 2 minutes, builds a page that brings calls.

## How it works

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  WORKS ALONE                                              │
│  ✓ Describe the job + photos. Get a post.                 │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  BETTER WITH                                              │
│  + Voice Card  + customer's OK to post                    │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## What I need from you

1. What the job was, and how long it took.
2. What the problem was before, what it looks like now.
3. The neighborhood (not the street address).
4. Customer's OK to post (yes/no). Describe your photos: before, after, angle.

## Steps

**Step 1. Check permission.** If the customer did not say yes, I will write the post without names, house numbers or faces, and tell you to ask first.

**Step 2. Write the post** with this structure: **Before problem → What we did → After result → How to call you.**

**Step 3. Choose a length for each place:**

| Platform | Max words | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Facebook / Instagram | 50 | Hook line first, 3 hashtags max |
| Google Business | 40 | Plain, no emoji, include service area |
| Nextdoor | 60 | Neighborly, name the neighborhood, no hard sell |

**Step 4. Photo advice:** which photos, order (before first, then after), same angle.

## Output

```
FACEBOOK / INSTAGRAM:
[Hook: the before problem or the after result]
[What we did — 1 line]
[Time it took, if short]
[Call or message for a free estimate — phone]
#[neighborhood] #[trade] #[one more]

GOOGLE POST: [plain version]
NEXTDOOR: [neighborly version]

PHOTOS: [order and angle advice]

⚠️ BEFORE YOU POST:
- [Customer permission]
- [Claims I could not verify]
- [Anything in the photo that shows a house number, plate, or face]
```

## Rules (and why)

- **Real photos only.** Never post a photo of another contractor's work. People can tell, and it ends your reputation.
- **Same angle for before and after.** A matching angle proves the change. A different angle looks like a trick.
- **Do not show the house number, license plates, or faces.** It protects the customer. It also protects you.
- **Say how long it took if it was fast.** "Done in 2 days" is a reason to call you.
- **Do not claim "licensed", "insured" or "best" unless true and you can show it.** Customers and the law check.
- **Do not post prices unless you want to be held to them.**

## Worked example

Messy input: *"redid front steps for a customer in baldwin, they were cracked concrete, did new brick veneer + railing, 3 days, she said ok to post, got before n after pics same spot"*

Output:
```
FACEBOOK / INSTAGRAM:
Cracked concrete steps → new brick steps with a railing. 🧱
Done in 3 days for a homeowner in Baldwin.
Safer to walk on, and the first thing guests see.
Need steps fixed? Call [phone] for a free estimate.
#Baldwin #masonry #longisland

GOOGLE POST:
Front steps rebuilt with brick veneer and a new railing in Baldwin, Long Island. 3 days. Call [phone] for a free estimate.

NEXTDOOR:
Hi neighbors in Baldwin. We just finished new front steps for a neighbor: the old concrete was cracked, now it is brick with a railing. Three days of work. If your steps need a look, we are happy to give a free estimate. [Name], [phone]

PHOTOS: before first, then after, same spot. Check the after photo does not show the house number.

⚠️ BEFORE YOU POST:
- "Safer to walk on": claim from me, not from you. Keep it only if you agree.
- "Free estimate": only if you really do free estimates.
```

## Tips

- Take the before photo on day 1, even if you think it is boring. After the work, you cannot go back.
- Ask the customer for one line: "Can I quote you?" A real quote is better than any line I write.
- Post when the customer is home. They often like, comment, and share to neighbors.
