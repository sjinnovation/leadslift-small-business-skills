# Job Update Texts — no surprises

Customers do not leave over problems. They leave over surprises and silence. A short text with a fact and a time keeps their trust, even when the news is bad.

## How it works

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  WORKS ALONE                                              │
│  ✓ Tell me what happened. Get a text ready to send.       │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  BETTER WITH                                              │
│  + Voice Card  + the original estimate                    │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## What I need from you

What happened on the job, and what the customer needs to know or do. If there is a delay, tell me the real new date. If there is extra cost, tell me the number.

## Steps

**Step 1. Pick the type:**

| Type | Send when | Must include |
|---|---|---|
| Start reminder | Evening before | Arrival window, who comes, what to clear |
| Running late | As soon as you know | New time. Say it before they ask |
| Delay | As soon as you know | Reason (one line), new date, what you are doing |
| Problem found | Same day | What you found, photo, options, price, your advice |
| Extra cost | Before you do the work | Reason, price, what happens if they say no, "reply YES" |
| Finished | End of job | What was done, cleanup done, final walk-through time |
| After the job | 2 days later | "Is everything ok?", then a review request |

**Step 2. Write the text.** Max 50 words. One topic per text.

**Step 3. Flag-back** for you only.

## Output

```
TEXT (send this):
Hi [Name], it is [Your name] from [Business].
[The fact, first sentence]
[New date/time or price]
[What you need from them — reply YES / choose A or B]

⚠️ BEFORE YOU SEND:
- [Facts I assumed]
- [Photos to attach]
- [Anything that needs a written approval]
```

## Rules (and why)

- **Bad news first, the same day.** Customers forgive a delay told on day 1. They do not forgive a delay found on day 5.
- **Always give a new date, even an honest range.** "Soon" makes them call. "Thursday or Friday" makes them wait.
- **Extra cost: ask before, never after.** "It was needed" after the work starts a fight. A text with a photo, a price and "reply YES" is a written approval.
- **One photo with every problem.** It proves you are not making it up.
- **Do not blame the customer, the supplier, or the weather by name.** Say what happened and what you are doing about it.
- **Ask for the review only after the customer is happy.** Not in the same message as bad news.

## Worked example

Messy input: *"mr chen bathroom tile. found rotted subfloor under the old tub. need to replace about 20 sq ft, extra 380. thats 1 extra day. i have photos"*

Output:
```
TEXT (send this):
Hi Mr. Chen, it is [Name] from [Business].
When I removed the old tub, I found a rotted section of subfloor, about 20 sq ft. I attached photos. It must be replaced before we tile, or the new floor will not last.
Cost: $380 extra and 1 more day. New finish date: [date].
Reply YES and I will fix it tomorrow. Call me if you have questions.

⚠️ BEFORE YOU SEND:
- Attach the photos.
- "1 more day" — I assumed same crew. Confirm the new date.
- Keep his YES text as your written approval.
```

## Tips

- Send "we start tomorrow" every time. It reduces no-access problems to nearly zero.
- Text, then call for anything over $200. A voice stops 10 follow-up texts.
- Keep the thread. Your phone is your best record.
