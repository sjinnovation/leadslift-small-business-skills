# Connectors — optional

Every skill works with **nothing connected**. You paste your text, and the AI gives you a draft. Connecting your tools only makes it faster.

## Placeholders used in the skills

| Placeholder | Means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Customer message | Text, email, DM or review you paste | WhatsApp, Gmail, Google review |
| Your prices | Your price list or menu | A photo or note you paste |
| Your calendar | Your open times | Google Calendar, Booksy, Square |

## If you want to connect

- **Claude:** connect Gmail or Google Calendar in your Claude settings so it can read your open times. Menu names change, so check Claude's help page.
- **ChatGPT:** use the same idea with its connected apps.

## Rules that never change

- AI **drafts**. You review and send.
- Do not connect tools that hold card numbers or health records.
- Start with nothing connected. Add one tool only after the skill works for you.
