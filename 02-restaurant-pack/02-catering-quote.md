# Catering Quote — clear price, clear yes

Catering is your best money per order. Most catering is lost to the first restaurant that sends a clear quote. A clear quote in 1 hour beats a better quote tomorrow.

## How it works

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  WORKS ALONE                                              │
│  ✓ Paste the request + your prices. Get a quote.          │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  BETTER WITH                                              │
│  + Voice Card  + your catering menu with prices           │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## What I need from you

1. The customer's request (date, number of people, food, place, special needs).
2. YOUR prices: per tray or per person. I will NOT invent prices.
3. Your rules: deposit, delivery fee, minimum order, how many days' notice.

## Steps

**Step 1. Check the request.** Find these 6 items. If any is missing, ask for it in the quote (one question only):
1. Date and time
2. Number of guests
3. Pickup or delivery (address)
4. Food choices
5. Allergies or diet needs
6. Budget (optional)

**Step 2. Count the food.** Use these starting amounts. They are common guides; use yours if different:

| Item | Per guest |
|---|---|
| Main dish (rice + protein) | 1 portion (about 8 to 10 oz) |
| Side dish | 1/2 portion each, 2 sides |
| Appetizer (if no main) | 3 to 4 pieces |
| Drink | 1 to 1.5 |
| Add 10% extra if guests are over 40 | to cover extra plates |

**Step 3. Write the quote** (template below). Offer 2 options: **Good** (fits budget) and **Better** (adds one thing).

**Step 4. Add the next step:** one way to say yes, and a date by which the quote ends.

## Output

```
Hi [Name],
Thank you for thinking of [Restaurant] for your [event] on [date].

Here is your quote for [N] guests:

OPTION 1 — [Name]
- [Dish — amount]
- [Dish — amount]
- [Dish — amount]
Price: $[total]  ($[per person] per person)

OPTION 2 — [Name] (adds [one thing])
Price: $[total]

Delivery: [yes/no, fee $___]   Setup: [yes/no]
Deposit: [your rule] to hold the date.
This quote is good until [date]. To confirm, reply YES and tell me your preferred option.

[Goodbye]

⚠️ BEFORE YOU SEND:
- [Missing details I asked about]
- [Prices that I took from you]
- [Allergy items to double-check]
```

## Rules (and why)

- **Total price AND per-person price.** Customers compare per person. Planners need the total.
- **Two options, not five.** Many choices make people delay. Two choices make people pick.
- **An end date on every quote.** Food prices and your schedule change. Without a date, a quote from last month comes back to bite you.
- **Put allergies in writing.** "Please tell us any allergies" protects the guest and you.
- **Never promise a date I was not given.** Check your kitchen first.

## Worked example

Messy input: *"hi need food for office lunch next thurs 25 ppl, chicken and veg, deliver to 37-10 main st, budget maybe 400. we have 1 vegan and 1 nut allergy"*
Your prices: *"chicken tray (feeds 10) $85, veg tray (feeds 10) $70, rice tray (feeds 10) $40, delivery $15, deposit 50%"*

Output:
```
OPTION 1 — Office Lunch
- Chicken tray x3 (30 portions)  $255
- Veg tray x2 (20 portions, includes vegan option)  $140
- Rice tray x3  $120
Price: $515 ($20.60 per person)  — over budget by $115

OPTION 2 — Budget fit
- Chicken tray x2  $170
- Veg tray x1  $70
- Rice tray x2  $80
Price: $320 + delivery $15 = $335 ($13.40 per person) — feeds 25 lighter portions
```

⚠️ BEFORE YOU SEND:
- Which Thursday? "next thurs" — confirm the date.
- Nut allergy: confirm which dishes are nut-free before you promise anything.
- Option 2 feeds only about 20 full portions for 25 people. Say so, or add a tray.

## Tips

- Send the quote the same day, even if rough. Speed signals care.
- Ask for the deposit at the same time. A paid deposit is the only real yes.
- Save the quote. Next time the same office orders, you reply in 2 minutes.
