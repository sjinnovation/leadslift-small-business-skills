# Daily Specials Post — 2 minutes, before the lunch rush

A special nobody knows about does not sell, and then the food is waste. A short post by 10 AM brings in the lunch crowd.

## How it works

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  WORKS ALONE                                              │
│  ✓ Type the specials. Get a post for each platform.       │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  BETTER WITH                                              │
│  + Voice Card  + photo of the dish                        │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## What I need from you

For each special: dish name, price, what is in it, until when (today only? until sold out?). Optional: photo description and your Voice Card.

## Steps

**Step 1.** Put the specials in this order: the one you most want to sell first. Why: readers remember the first line.

**Step 2.** Write the post for each platform:

| Platform | Max words | Style |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram / Facebook | 45 | One hook line, dishes with prices, time, address |
| Google Business post | 40 | Plain, no emoji, include hours |
| WhatsApp / text blast | 30 | Very short, one dish, price, "reply to reserve" |

**Step 3.** Add the "sold out" version, ready to post if you run out.

## Output

```
INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK:
[Hook with the main dish]
[Dish 1 — $price — one tasty detail]
[Dish 2 — $price]
Today only. [Hours]. [Address / area]

GOOGLE POST:
[Plain version]

WHATSAPP:
[Short version]

SOLD OUT VERSION:
[Dish] is sold out. Thank you! Tomorrow: [if known]

Photo idea: [one line]

⚠️ BEFORE YOU POST:
- [Allergen or ingredient claims I did not verify]
- [Anything you may run out of]
```

## Rules (and why)

- **Prices on every dish.** A post without prices makes people ask, and most people do not ask. They scroll.
- **Say "today only" only if true.** Fake urgency loses trust fast, and regulars notice.
- **Never claim "gluten-free", "nut-free", "halal", "vegan" or "organic" unless you told me it is true.** A wrong claim can hurt a person. I will mark it for you to check.
- **Max 2 emoji.** The food photo is the star.
- **Always include the area or street.** Many customers find you while walking.

## Worked example

Messy input: *"today: chicken biryani 12.99 w raita, goat curry 15 ill run out by 2pm, mango lassi 4. open till 10. we are on 74th st"*

Output:
```
INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK:
Chicken biryani day! 🍛
Chicken biryani with raita — $12.99
Goat curry — $15 (we run out by 2 PM)
Mango lassi — $4
Today only. Open until 10 PM. 74th St.

GOOGLE POST:
Today's specials: chicken biryani with raita $12.99, goat curry $15, mango lassi $4. Open until 10 PM on 74th St.

WHATSAPP:
Chicken biryani + raita today, $12.99. Open till 10 PM, 74th St. Reply to save a plate.

SOLD OUT VERSION:
Goat curry is sold out. Thank you! Biryani is still available until 10 PM.

⚠️ BEFORE YOU POST:
- "Run out by 2 PM" — I used your words. Edit if kitchen changes.
- Is the goat curry halal? I did not say it. Add only if true.
```

## Tips

- Post at 10 AM for lunch, 3 PM for dinner. People decide 2 hours before they eat.
- Keep a "regular special" list (Monday biryani). Paste it once, ask for the week.
- Post the sold-out message too. It builds a "sells fast" feeling that is true.
