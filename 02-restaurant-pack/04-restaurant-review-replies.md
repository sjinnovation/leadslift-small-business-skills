# Restaurant Review Replies

Diners read the owner's reply before they book. How you answer a 1-star review tells new guests how you treat them. This skill is for restaurants. For the general rules, see Starter pack skill 4.

## How it works

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  WORKS ALONE                                              │
│  ✓ Paste the review. Get a reply to post.                 │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  BETTER WITH                                              │
│  + Voice Card  + your side of the story, in 2 lines       │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## What I need from you

The review, the star rating, and your side (what really happened). Say if it came through a delivery app.

## Steps

**Step 1. Pick the restaurant case:**

| Case | What the reply must do | Never |
|---|---|---|
| Slow service | Sorry once, say what changed (staff, process) | Blame a person by name |
| Food cold or wrong | Sorry, say you will fix, invite offline contact | Say "you ordered wrong" |
| Allergy or sickness | Take it seriously, ask them to call you today, no excuses | Argue, guess the cause, or say "not our fault" |
| Hair, bug, dirty claim | Calm, ask for contact so you can look into it | Deny in public or insult the guest |
| Delivery app problem | Say the app handled delivery, say you will check the order | Blame the driver by name |
| Rude staff | Sorry, say you spoke with the team (only if true) | Name the staff |
| Praise | Thank by name, mention the dish, share the praise with the team | Copy-paste |

**Step 2. Write the reply.** Max 60 words for 1 to 3 stars, 40 for 4 to 5 stars.

**Step 3. Flag-back for the owner.**

## Output

```
REPLY (post this):
[Thanks / sorry, with first name]
[One real detail from the review]
[What happened or what you changed, one sentence]
[Offline: phone or email]
— [Name], [Restaurant]

⚠️ BEFORE YOU POST:
- [Statements I could not verify]
- [Whether to talk to the staff member privately]
- [Whether to report the review to the platform]
```

## Rules (and why)

- **Allergy or sickness reviews: reply within 24 hours and ask for a call.** These can be serious for the guest. Do not guess the cause in public. If a guest says they got sick, take it as real.
- **No excuses in public.** "We were busy" reads as "we do not care". Say what you changed.
- **Never name staff.** It humiliates people, and good people leave.
- **Never share the order or visit time.** Private details can break trust.
- **Report fake reviews** (never visited, wrong restaurant) on the platform. Reply calmly once.

## Worked example

Messy input: *"1 star. ordered chicken biryani on doordash, came cold and rice was hard. never again. - Tom"*
Your side: *"Delivery took 55 min per the app. Our kitchen sent it hot."*

Output:
```
REPLY (post this):
Hi Tom, I am sorry your biryani arrived cold and hard. That is not how we want it served.
Your order took a long time to reach you through the delivery app, and we are checking how we pack for long trips.
Please email me at [email]. I would like to make this right.
— [Name], [Restaurant]

⚠️ BEFORE YOU POST:
- "We are checking how we pack" — only post if you will really do it.
- I did not say the kitchen sent it hot. That sounds like blame and Tom cannot check it.
- Hard rice may be a kitchen issue too. Taste test a delivery order.
```

## Tips

- Once a month, copy 5 bad reviews into a list. If 3 say the same thing, that is your next fix.
- A reply to an old bad review still helps. Readers scroll.
- Thank servers in your good-review replies by "our team". Staff read them too.
