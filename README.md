# LeadsLift Small Business AI Skills

Free AI skills for small business owners. Ready-made prompts for customer replies, social posts, reviews, estimates and more. Works in Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini. Packs for restaurants, salons and contractors.

Each skill is one file. You paste your real details (a customer message, a price). The AI writes a draft. **You read it and send it.** Nothing sends by itself.

**New here? Read [START-HERE.md](START-HERE.md).** Start with the Starter pack, skill 1.

## Use it

- **Claude:** use the whole skill file.
- **ChatGPT or Gemini:** use the "Quick paste version" at the bottom of each file.
- Details are in [START-HERE.md](START-HERE.md). Email, calendar and CRM tips are in [CONNECTORS.md](CONNECTORS.md).

## Good to know

- You need your own AI account. LeadsLift does not supply it.
- Skills give drafts and guides. They are not legal, medical, tax or financial advice.
- Never paste card numbers, passwords or ID numbers into an AI tool.
