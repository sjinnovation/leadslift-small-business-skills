# Booking Replies — answer between clients

Your hands are busy all day. A client who waits 3 hours for "how much?" books somewhere else. This skill gives you fast replies that end with a booked time.

## How it works

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  WORKS ALONE                                              │
│  ✓ Paste the message + your open times. Get a reply.      │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  BETTER WITH                                              │
│  + Voice Card  + your price list  + your cancel policy    │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## What I need from you

1. The client message.
2. Your open times (or say "I will fill in").
3. Your prices and your cancel policy (paste once per chat). I will not guess prices.

## Steps

**Step 1. Type of message:**

| Type | Reply must |
|---|---|
| Price | Give the price or a range, and what it includes, then offer 2 times |
| Availability | Offer exactly 2 times (not 5, not "anytime") |
| Reschedule | Offer 2 new times, state your change rule |
| Cancel | Confirm, state any fee only if your policy says so, offer to rebook |
| Running late | State how long you can wait (your rule) and the choice: come or rebook |
| First visit | Say how long it takes, what to bring, where to park or enter |
| Photo request ("can you do this?") | Say yes/no honestly, price range, ask for a consult if hair needs a check |

**Step 2. Write the reply.** Max 50 words. Use the client's first name.

**Step 3. Flag-back for you.**

## Output

```
REPLY (copy this):
Hi [Name],
[Answer in first sentence: price / yes / sorry]
[Time option 1] or [Time option 2] — which works?
[Anything they need: how long, deposit, address]

⚠️ BEFORE YOU SEND:
- [Prices I used from you]
- [Open times to confirm]
- [Anything outside your policy]
```

## Rules (and why)

- **Offer exactly 2 times.** "When are you free?" starts a long chat. Two choices end it in one reply.
- **Price first, then the time.** A client who asked "how much?" and got "what day?" feels dodged.
- **Say how long the service takes.** People plan their day around it. Missing this causes late arrivals.
- **State your policy in a friendly way before it is needed.** "I hold your spot for 15 minutes" in a booking message is easier than saying it at the door.
- **Never guarantee a color or result from a photo.** Hair differs. Say "I can get close, I will tell you at the consult". It protects you from a bad review.
- **No health or medical claims** for spa services.

## Worked example

Messy input: *"hi how much for box braids shoulder length? and do u have anything this sat"*
Your info: *"box braids shoulder length $180, takes about 5 hours, Sat open 9 AM and 1 PM, $40 deposit"*

Output:
```
REPLY (copy this):
Hi! Shoulder-length box braids are $180 and take about 5 hours.
This Saturday I have 9 AM or 1 PM. Which do you want?
A $40 deposit holds your spot. Please bring your hair washed and blow-dried.

⚠️ BEFORE YOU SEND:
- "Washed and blow-dried": I assumed this. Remove if you do it for them.
- 1 PM + 5 hours ends at 6 PM. Check you can close at that time.
- I did not ask hair length. Confirm "shoulder length" with a photo.
```

## Tips

- Save your 5 most common answers in your phone notes. Paste into the chat to personalize.
- When a client goes quiet after you give a price, send one message 2 days later: "Hi [Name], still want that spot? I have [time]." Then stop.
- Put "I reply between clients, within 2 hours" in your profile. Calm clients wait.
