# No-Show Follow-Up — keep the client, protect the chair

A no-show costs you the money for that slot, and it cannot be sold again. But a harsh message loses the client forever. The right message rebooks most clients and teaches the rest.

## How it works

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  WORKS ALONE                                              │
│  ✓ Tell me what happened. Get 1 to 2 messages.            │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  BETTER WITH                                              │
│  + Voice Card  + your no-show policy (fee? deposit?)      │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## What I need from you

1. What happened: no-show, late cancel, or gone quiet.
2. How many times it happened with this client (1st, 2nd, 3rd).
3. Your policy (deposit, fee, grace time). If none, I will suggest a simple one and mark it as a suggestion.

## Steps

**Step 1. Pick the sequence:**

| Situation | Message 1 | Message 2 | Stop |
|---|---|---|---|
| First no-show | Within 2 hours: kind, "is everything ok?", offer 2 new times | Day 3: only if no reply, one rebook offer | After message 2 |
| Second no-show | Within 2 hours: kind, state the policy once, offer a rebook with a deposit | None | After message 1 |
| Third no-show | One short message: you can book again only with a prepaid deposit | None | After message 1 |
| Late cancel (under 24 hours) | Thank them for telling you, state the policy, offer 2 new times | None | After message 1 |
| Gone quiet (6+ weeks) | Day 0: "We miss you" + a real reason to return | Day 7: one offer | After message 2 |

**Step 2. Write the messages.** Max 40 words each.

**Step 3. Flag-back.**

## Output

```
MESSAGE 1 (send [timing]):
Hi [Name], [kind first line]. [Offer 2 times / state policy]. [One easy action].

MESSAGE 2 (send [timing], only if no reply):
[Short second line]

⚠️ BEFORE YOU SEND:
- [Policy I used from you / my suggestion]
- [Whether to charge a fee — your decision]
```

## Rules (and why)

- **First no-show: assume something happened.** Most clients forgot or had an emergency. If you assume the worst, you lose a regular for a rule.
- **Maximum 2 messages. Then stop.** A third looks like chasing. It makes people block you.
- **State the policy once, kindly, and in writing.** A rule that was never told is not a rule. A rule said once in a friendly way is respected.
- **Never name the lost money in the message.** "You cost me $120" makes clients feel attacked. Say "that slot could have helped another client".
- **Use deposits for repeat no-shows.** A deposit is the only thing that changes behavior. You decide the amount.
- **I do not advise on fees or legal terms.** Check your local rules.

## Worked example

Messy input: *"tanya no show today 3pm balayage, 2nd time. i dont want to lose her she is good client"*

Output:
```
MESSAGE 1 (send within 2 hours):
Hi Tanya, we missed you at 3 PM today. I hope everything is ok.
I would love to see you. I have Thursday 11 AM or Saturday 2 PM.
Since this is the 2nd time, I now hold balayage spots with a $[amount] deposit. It goes toward your service. Which time works?

⚠️ BEFORE YOU SEND:
- The deposit: you did not give an amount. Fill in your number.
- This is a suggestion, not a policy you told me. Use it only if you want it.
- "2nd time": check your records.
```

## Tips

- Send reminders 24 hours and 2 hours before. This stops many no-shows before they happen.
- A client who no-shows once and gets a kind message often becomes your most loyal.
- Write your policy on your booking page and in the confirmation. Clients cannot say "I did not know".
