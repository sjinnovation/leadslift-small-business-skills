# Promo of the Week — fill the empty chairs

An empty chair earns $0. A small, smart offer on a slow day beats a big discount on a busy day. One promo, one week, one message. No complicated deals.

## How it works

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  WORKS ALONE                                              │
│  ✓ Tell me your slow days. Get 1 promo + the messages.    │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  BETTER WITH                                              │
│  + Voice Card  + your prices and service costs            │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## What I need from you

1. Which days or times are empty this week.
2. Your service prices.
3. The lowest price or discount you can accept without losing money. If you do not know, I will ask you 2 questions.

## Steps

**Step 1. Pick the promo type for the problem:**

| Problem | Promo | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Empty slow day | Add-on on that day only | "Tuesday: free scalp massage with any cut" |
| Last-minute open slot | Same-day price | "Open today 3 PM: $[price]" |
| Old clients gone | Come-back offer with a date | "Back by [date]: $[amount] off" |
| Add-on sales low | Bundle | "Cut + beard trim, $[price]" |
| Holiday | Gift or package | "Gift card $50, get $[bonus]" |

**Step 2. Check the math.** Show: normal price, promo price, what you give up. Rule: **never discount more than 20%** unless you are sure of your cost. An add-on or bonus costs you less than a discount and looks like more value.

**Step 3. Write 3 messages:** Instagram/Facebook post (max 40 words), text to past clients (max 30 words), and a sign line for the door (max 10 words).

## Output

```
PROMO: [name]
When: [days/times only]
Offer: [one sentence]
Normal price $[ ] → Promo $[ ]  (you give up about $[ ] per client)
End date: [date]

POST: [text]
TEXT TO PAST CLIENTS: [text]
DOOR SIGN: [text]

⚠️ BEFORE YOU POST:
- [Math I assumed]
- [Rules to write clearly: limit, expiry, one per client]
```

## Rules (and why)

- **One promo at a time.** Two offers confuse people, and neither works.
- **A start and end date.** No end date means the discount becomes the normal price.
- **Only on slow times.** A promo on a busy Saturday gives away money you were going to earn anyway.
- **Prefer an add-on to a discount.** A free scalp massage costs you 10 minutes. A 20% discount costs money. Clients value both.
- **Limit it.** "First 5 clients" or "one per person" keeps it safe. Say it is true only if it is.
- **No health or "cure" claims** in spa promos.

## Worked example

Messy input: *"tuesdays dead. i do haircuts 35, beard trim 15. barbershop. 4 chairs mostly empty on tues"*

Output:
```
PROMO: Tuesday Cut + Trim
When: Tuesdays this week and next, all day
Offer: Haircut + beard trim for $45 (normal $50)
You give up $5 per client. A Tuesday client brings $45 you would not have.
End date: Tuesday 17 Nov

POST: Slow Tuesday? Not for you. Haircut + beard trim, $45 (usually $50). Tuesdays only, until Nov 17. Book a chair, 4 open all day.
TEXT: Hi [Name], Tuesday special: cut + beard trim $45, usual $50. Until Nov 17. Want a time?
DOOR SIGN: Tuesday: cut + trim $45.

⚠️ BEFORE YOU POST:
- "4 open all day" — only write it if true. Check the book.
- Barbers pay: check this does not cut into your barbers' share. Talk to them first.
```

## Tips

- Ask: "Which service has the longest wait for a client to come back?" Make that the promo.
- Count how many redeemed it. If 0, change the offer, not the price.
- Send the text to past clients first. They respond faster than strangers.
