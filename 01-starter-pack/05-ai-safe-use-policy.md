# AI Safe-Use Policy — one page, 10 rules

Your staff already use AI. Without rules, someone will paste a customer's card number or send an AI answer nobody checked. One page of clear rules prevents most problems. This is a practical guide, not legal advice. For legal questions, ask a lawyer.

## How it works

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  WORKS ALONE                                              │
│  ✓ Answer 6 questions. Get a one-page policy.             │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  BETTER WITH                                              │
│  + Your current staff handbook, if you have one           │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## What I need from you

Answer these 6 (one at a time):
1. Business type and number of staff?
2. Which AI tools do staff use? (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, other)
3. Is the AI account paid and owned by the business, or personal accounts?
4. What customer data do you hold? (names, phones, cards, health, ID, photos)
5. Who is the "AI owner" — the person staff ask when unsure?
6. Does anything go out to customers (replies, posts, quotes)? Who checks it?

## Steps

**Step 1.** Choose the risk level from the answers:

| Signal | Level | Extra rule to add |
|---|---|---|
| Holds health, ID, or card data | High | "No customer data in AI. Ever." |
| Holds names and phones only | Medium | "Remove names and phones before pasting." |
| Little customer data | Low | Base rules only |
| Personal AI accounts | Any | "Use the business account only." |

**Step 2.** Write the policy using the template.

**Step 3.** Add a 5-line staff signup (name, date, "I read this").

## Output

```
[BUSINESS NAME] — AI SAFE-USE RULES
Version [date]. Questions go to: [AI owner name and phone].

1. Use only the business AI account: [tool name].
2. Never paste: customer card numbers, ID numbers, passwords, health details, full addresses.
3. Remove customer names and phone numbers before pasting [if Medium].
4. A person reads every AI answer before it goes to a customer. No exceptions.
5. Check every price, date, address, and promise in an AI answer. AI makes things up.
6. Do not use AI to write fake reviews or fake customer stories.
7. Do not use AI to make a decision about a person (hiring, firing, pay) alone.
8. Say it is AI if a customer asks.
9. If you paste something by mistake, tell [AI owner] the same day. No blame for telling early.
10. When unsure, ask. [AI owner] answers within 1 working day.

[Extra rule for High risk: "No customer data in AI. Ever."]

Staff: I read this. Name ______ Date ______

⚠️ BEFORE YOU USE:
- [Things I assumed — please check]
- [Laws that may apply to your business — ask a lawyer]
```

## Rules (and why)

- **Max 10 rules, one page.** Staff read one page. They skip five pages.
- **Rule 9 has "no blame".** If people fear blame, they hide mistakes. Hidden mistakes cost more.
- **Name one real person as AI owner.** "Management" is nobody. A name is someone to ask.
- **Say "not legal advice".** Some businesses (health, finance, children) have special laws. A lawyer must check those.
- **Do not invent laws.** I only write rules of conduct. I will not claim what the law says.

## Worked example

Messy input: *"salon 6 staff chatgpt free on their phones we have names phones and photos of customers hair. maria manager"*

Output (short):
```
GLOW SALON — AI SAFE-USE RULES
Version 6 Nov 2026. Questions go to: Maria (manager).

1. Use only the salon ChatGPT account. No personal accounts.
2. Never paste: card numbers, passwords, full addresses.
3. Remove customer names and phone numbers before pasting.
4. A person reads every AI answer before a customer sees it.
...
5. Never upload customer photos to AI without the customer's yes.
```

⚠️ BEFORE YOU USE:
- Staff use free personal accounts now. Create one business account first, or rule 1 cannot work.
- Photos of customers: ask a lawyer if you need written permission in your state.

## Tips

- Read the 10 rules aloud at your next team meeting. It takes 3 minutes. Spoken rules get remembered.
- Review the policy every 6 months. AI tools change fast.
- A new hire signs it on day 1, with the same pen as the tax forms.

## Quick paste version for ChatGPT

> You are helping me write a one-page AI safe-use policy for my small business staff. This is a practical guide, not legal advice. Ask me these 6 questions one at a time: business type and staff count; AI tools used; business or personal accounts; what customer data we hold; who is the AI owner; what goes out to customers and who checks it. Then write max 10 simple rules, with a name for the AI owner, a "no blame if you tell us early" rule, and a staff sign-line. Add stricter rules if we hold health, ID, or card data. Do not claim what the law says. End with a "Before you use" list for me.
