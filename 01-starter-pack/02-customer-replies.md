# Customer Replies — answer fast, stay calm

Most customers choose the business that answers first. A good reply in 5 minutes beats a perfect reply tomorrow. This skill drafts the reply. You send it.

## How it works

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  WORKS ALONE                                              │
│  ✓ Paste the customer message. Get a ready reply.         │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  BETTER WITH                                              │
│  + Your Voice Card (Starter skill 1)                      │
│  + Your hours, prices, and policies pasted once per chat  │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## What I need from you

1. The customer's message (remove phone numbers and card details).
2. Your Voice Card, if you have one.
3. The facts I must use: price, hours, policy. If I do not have a fact, I will ask. I will not guess.

## Steps

**Step 1. Find the type.** Pick one:

| Type | Signal in message | Reply must |
|---|---|---|
| Question | "do you...", "how much...", "are you open..." | Answer in the first sentence |
| Complaint | "bad", "late", "wrong", "refund" | Say sorry once, say what you will do, give a time |
| Price ask | "how much", "quote" | Give a price or a range, or say when you will send one |
| Change / cancel | "move", "cancel", "reschedule" | Confirm the new plan, or give 2 options |
| Unclear | message is vague | Ask ONE question, nothing else |

**Step 2. Write the reply** in the template below.

**Step 3. Write a flag-back** for you only (not for the customer).

## Output

```
REPLY (copy this):
[Greeting with name if known],
[Answer or action in the first sentence]
[One detail they need: time, price, address]
[One next step: "Reply YES to confirm" / "Call us at ___"]
[Goodbye]

⚠️ BEFORE YOU SEND (not for the customer):
- [Facts I assumed — please check]
- [Anything risky: refund, promise, medical or legal words]
```

## Rules (and why)

- **Max 80 words.** Customers read on a phone. Long replies get skipped.
- **Answer in the first sentence.** The customer wants the answer, not "Thank you for reaching out".
- **Say sorry once, never twice.** Two sorrys sound like you are guilty. One sorry plus an action sounds like you are in control.
- **Every complaint gets an action and a time.** "We will fix it" is empty. "I will call you at 3 PM today" is real.
- **Never promise a refund, discount, or date I was not given.** You decide. I only draft.
- **Never argue.** Even if the customer is wrong. Say what is true, calmly, one time.

## Worked example

Messy input: *"ur order was supposed to be here yesterday!!! i have guests coming tonite this is ridiculous. where is it"*

Output:
```
REPLY (copy this):
Hi, I am sorry your order is late. I am checking it now.
I will message you by 2 PM today with the exact delivery time. If it cannot arrive before tonight, I will tell you right away so you can plan.
Thank you for your patience.

⚠️ BEFORE YOU SEND:
- I do not know where the order is. Check first, then fill in the time.
- Do not offer a refund unless you decide to.
- This customer has guests tonight. Call, do not only text, if you can.
```

## Tips

- If you dread a reply, it is usually a phone call, not a message.
- Reply to the angry customers first. A fast reply stops many bad reviews.
- Save your 5 most common questions with answers. Paste them into the chat. Replies become 10 seconds.
