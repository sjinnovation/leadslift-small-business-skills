# Review Responses — every review gets an answer

People read your replies to bad reviews more than the bad review itself. A calm reply wins customers you never met. This skill drafts the reply. You post it.

## How it works

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  WORKS ALONE                                              │
│  ✓ Paste the review. Get a reply ready to post.           │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  BETTER WITH                                              │
│  + Your Voice Card  + what really happened (your side)    │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## What I need from you

1. The review text and star rating.
2. (For 1 to 3 stars) What really happened, in 1 or 2 lines.
3. Your Voice Card, if you have one.

## Steps

**Step 1. Pick the case.**

| Stars | Reply must | Max words |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Thank by name, mention ONE thing they said, invite them back | 40 |
| 4 | Same as 5, plus ask what would make it 5 | 50 |
| 3 | Thank, say sorry for the miss, name one fix | 60 |
| 1 to 2, true | Say sorry once, say what you changed, offer to talk offline | 60 |
| 1 to 2, false or unfair | State the fact calmly, once, offer to talk offline | 60 |
| Fake (not a customer) | Stay calm, say you cannot find the order, ask them to contact you | 40 |

**Step 2. Write the reply.**

**Step 3. Flag-back for you only.**

## Output

```
REPLY (post this):
[Thanks / sorry, with name]
[One real detail from their review]
[What you did or will do — 1 sentence]
[Invite: "Please call us at ___" or "We hope to see you again"]
— [Your first name], [Business name]

⚠️ BEFORE YOU POST:
- [Anything that may not be true]
- [Any private info about the customer — remove it]
- [Whether to report the review as fake]
```

## Rules (and why)

- **Reply within 24 hours for 1 to 3 stars.** A fast reply shows other readers you care. A slow reply looks like you do not.
- **Never argue in public.** Readers judge you, not the customer. Answer once, calmly, then take it offline.
- **Never share private details** (order, name, visit time) in a bad-review reply. It can break trust and sometimes rules.
- **Do not copy the same reply twice.** Customers and review sites notice. Change one real detail each time.
- **Do not offer money in public.** "Contact us and we will make it right" is enough.
- **Do not ask for or buy 5-star reviews in exchange for a gift.** Platforms can remove your reviews. Ask happy customers, nothing more.

## Worked example

Messy input: *"2 stars. waited 40 min for food, waiter was rude, food ok tho. Maria"*
Your side: *"Friday night, we had 2 cooks out sick. Waiter was new."*

Output:
```
REPLY (post this):
Hi Maria, thank you for telling us. 40 minutes is too long, and I am sorry.
We were short two cooks that Friday, and we should have told you the wait when you sat down. We now tell every table the wait time.
I would like to hear more. Please call me at [phone].
— [Name], [Business]

⚠️ BEFORE YOU POST:
- "We now tell every table the wait time" — only post this if it is true.
- I did not mention the waiter. Talk to your waiter privately.
- Do not say "new waiter" in public. It blames a person.
```

## Tips

- After a good visit, say in person: "If you liked it, a Google review helps us a lot." Real words beat a sign.
- Answer the 5-star reviews too. It takes 20 seconds and readers see an active owner.
- A review with 1 star and no text: reply "Sorry about your visit. Please contact us, we want to fix it."
