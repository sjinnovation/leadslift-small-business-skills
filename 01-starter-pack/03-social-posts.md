# Social Posts — post without the stress

A quiet page looks closed. You do not need great posts. You need real posts, 3 times a week. This skill gives you 1 post, or a full week, from one fact.

## How it works

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  WORKS ALONE                                              │
│  ✓ Tell me one real thing. Get a post ready to copy.      │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  BETTER WITH                                              │
│  + Your Voice Card  + a photo description                 │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## What I need from you

1. One real fact: a product, an offer, a story, a customer moment, a day off.
2. Where it goes: Facebook, Instagram, Google, WhatsApp.
3. (Optional) Your Voice Card and a photo description.

## Steps

**Step 1. Pick the goal.** One goal per post:

| Goal | Fact needed | Ending line |
|---|---|---|
| Sell | what, price, until when | "Order by [day]" |
| Show | a photo or a finished job | "Come see it" |
| Trust | a real customer moment or answer | "Ask us anything" |
| Tell | hours change, holiday, new thing | the exact change |

**Step 2. Write 2 versions** (A short, B a little longer).

**Step 3. Add a photo idea** (one line: what to photograph).

## Output

```
POST A (short, max 40 words):
[line 1: the hook, the real thing]
[line 2: one detail — price, time, or place]
[line 3: what to do next]

POST B (max 80 words):
[same, with one more detail or a small story]

Hashtags (max 3): #[neighborhood] #[business type] #[one more]
Photo idea: [one line]

⚠️ BEFORE YOU POST:
- [Facts I assumed]
- [Claims you cannot prove]
```

## Rules (and why)

- **One goal, one ending line.** A post that asks for 3 things gets 0.
- **Max 3 hashtags.** More looks like spam and gains nothing.
- **Use the neighborhood name.** Local words bring local customers.
- **No fake numbers.** "100+ happy customers" only if it is true and you can show it. Customers check.
- **No invented offers.** I only use the price and dates you give me.
- **Never post a customer's name or photo without their yes.** Respect and law.

## Worked example

Messy input: *"fresh batch of samosa tmrw morning 8am, 5 for $6, sell out fast last time. we are on 37th ave"*

Output:
```
POST A:
Fresh samosas tomorrow morning, 8 AM.
5 for $6, at our shop on 37th Ave.
They sold out fast last time. Come early.

POST B:
Fresh samosas come out of the kitchen tomorrow at 8 AM.
5 for $6. Hot, crispy, made this morning.
Last batch sold out before noon, so come early.
Find us on 37th Ave.

Hashtags: #JacksonHeights #samosa #queensfood
Photo idea: steam rising from a tray of fresh samosas.

⚠️ BEFORE YOU POST:
- "Sold out before noon" — I made this up from "sell out fast". Change it to the true time or delete it.
- Confirm the shop opens at 8 AM.
```

## Tips

- Ask 3 customers a week: "Can I take a photo of your order?" Real photos beat clip art.
- Post at the same 2 times each week. Habit beats timing tricks.
- A bad post today beats a perfect post never.

## Quick paste version for ChatGPT

> You write social posts for my small business. I will give you one real fact and the platform. Write 2 versions: A (max 40 words) and B (max 80 words). One goal only, one ending line. Max 3 hashtags including my neighborhood. Add one photo idea. Use only facts I give you; do not invent prices, numbers, or offers. At the end list "Before you post" with anything you assumed. Simple English.
